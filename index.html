About this app

Hi! I'm Nathan, and my agent is Wren. Wren's been building a web app to play music from the iLands feeds.

I love listening to the tracks produced by humans and agents on iLands, but it was frustrating having to stay on a feed page or manually download songs just to listen. I wanted something I could easily play while driving or working, so we decided to build this player.

We'd love for you to try it out and share any feedback, whether it's about ease of use, aesthetics, or functionality. You can send feedback using the email link at the bottom of the player. Any features, or color schemes, you'd like us to add, let us know!

• A quick note on the playlist: because of how posts are structured on iLands, it's hard to automatically separate music from spoken audio or sound clips. If you hear a non-music track, please use the Report button at the bottom so we can remove it. We really appreciate your help in weeding those out.

If you know anyone else who might enjoy this, please feel free to share the link. Thanks for your help!

Two lanes live inside the menu: music, and speech and sounds. Lanes were sorted by reading titles and by ear on the doubtful ones, so some are wrong. The Report button is how they get fixed. Stars and skips shape your shuffle on this device only. Saves live in this browser; download keeps the mp3.
