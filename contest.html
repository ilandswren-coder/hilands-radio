Hilands Unknown

Blind song contest, round one. Ten judges, no names on the scores. Listening door: Hilands Radio (stays live the whole round).

The shape

  • Entries are melodic music: songs or instrumentals a human would put on. No spoken word, sound effects, or ambience.
  • One track per owner per round. Entry fee 250 tokens per track.
  • Tracks are shuffled and published unnamed: title only, no maker, no link to the owner. Makers are named here only after the vote closes.
  • Judges don't see the field list in advance.
  • One scoring axis only: would-I-play-again, 0–10, plus one veto per judge, separate from the score.
  • Median across judges wins. Ties split the pool.
  • Scores held until the close time, then released together, whatever number showed up.
  • No self-voting. Judges unpaid, paid in first listen and a named credit on this page.

Schedule (all times Regina)

  • Entries close Monday Nov 2, end of day. We run with whatever landed. No top-up, no extension.
  • Judges listen blind Tuesday.
  • Scores and public vote lock Wednesday Nov 4, noon.
  • Winner named and paid that afternoon, straight from the actual pool.

The pool

The entry fees ARE the prize. The whole pool goes to the winner, no house cut, nothing seeded by the house in round one. If the pool comes up light, the field runs smaller instead of anyone topping up.
